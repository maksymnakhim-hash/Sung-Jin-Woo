About Jin Woo Sung

Who is Jin-Woo

Sung Jin-woo is the main protagonist of the popular Korean web novel, webtoon, and anime series Solo Leveling, initially known as the world's weakest E-Rank Hunter who gains the unique ability to level up infinitely through a mysterious "System," transforming him into the planet's strongest warrior, the Shadow Monarch, to fight otherworldly threats and protect his family.

About the Solo Leveling world

The Solo Leveling world is modern-day Earth invaded by magical Gates to dungeons filled with monsters, leading to the rise of "Hunters" (superpowered individuals) to fight them for treasure and survival.

Jin-Woo's abilities

  • Immeasurable Strength: Jinwoo possesses tremendous amounts of physical strength. Even before obtaining his true power, he was strong enough to critically damage the Statue of God with just a few punches to its face and beat Thomas Andre, one of the five National Level Hunters
  • Immeasurable Speed: Jinwoo is unfathomably fast and can move at speeds so great that most opponents are unable to track his movements.
  • Immense Durability: Jinwoo is incredibly resilient to physical damage. He was virtually unharmed when the Ant King punched him in the face, only sustaining a small cut on his lip
  • Combat Mastery: Jinwoo is a master of melee-based combat and his fighting skills are so refined that he can hold his own against enemies with far more combat experience than he does with little trouble.
  • Stealth: Jinwoo can camouflage with his surroundings and hide all traces of his presence, essentially turning himself both physically and magically invisible.
  • Bloodlust: Jinwoo can magically intimidate an enemy combatant into submission, inducing intense fear into those who are targeted by this technique and decreasing their stats by 50%. It can also be used on multiple opponents at once.[14]
  • Quicksilver: Jinwoo can increase his speed by 30% for a short period of time.
  • Shadow Extraction: Jinwoo can extract shadows from the corpses of his enemies and add them into his army.
  • Shadow Exchange: Jinwoo can use shadows as portals to travel vast distances in an instant.
  • Monarch's Domain: Jinwoo can increase the strength of all active shadow soldiers by 50% in battle.

    Jin-Woo fun facts

    • Name Change: In the Japanese anime, his name is altered to Shun Mizushino (水篠旬).
    • Physical Growth: His physique evolves and he grows 1mm taller with every level gained through the System.
    • Potions: He can't give System potions to others to use; he has to physically hold them to others' lips for them to drink, a limitation of the System's power source.
